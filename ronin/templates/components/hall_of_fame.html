選出 — How the Tenno is elected

No vote. No council. No inheritance. The ledger decides.

Mastery is measured as (honor earned ÷ total honor available) × 100. The warrior holding rank #1 on the honor scroll wears the crown — the moment they are overtaken, it passes to the challenger. Even at 100% mastery, only the single reigning #1 may call themselves Tenno.
